UNDERGRADUATE THESIS01

Physics-informed
digital twins

Investigating the limits of digital twins under transient loads in micro-mechatronic systems through hardware-in-the-loop experiments.

  • Reduced-order electromechanical modelling using equivalent electrical impedance.
  • A continuous Thermal Health Index from voltage, current, and temperature telemetry.
  • GRU-based residual analysis and time-to-threshold forecasting with Monte Carlo dropout.

Advisor: Dr. Md. Anwar Hossain
